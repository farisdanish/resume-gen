Catching public transit in Malaysia shouldn't require guessing when the next bus will show up. BasBuddy is an unofficial, mobile-first transit tracker delivering live vehicle positions, directional map tracking, and instant stop ETAs powered by open data from data.gov.my.

Parses static GTFS feeds into PostgreSQL; background poller decodes GTFS-RT protobuf feeds across multi-agency routes, snaps GPS to polyline shapes, and caches precalculated ETAs in Valkey to serve sub-millisecond responses to a React PWA.
